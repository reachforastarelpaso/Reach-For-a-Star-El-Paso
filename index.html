Our mission

Every child deserves a reason to smile

Reach for a Star El Paso grants wishes to critically ill children throughout West Texas and Southern New Mexico. A wish can't cure an illness, but it can bring a family together, lift a child's spirits and give them something wonderful to look forward to.

We're a local 501(c)(3) nonprofit, supported by neighbors, businesses and first responders who believe in the power of hope.
